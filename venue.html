Getting there

Arriving from

Ernakulam

From MG Road or Marine Drive, head to High Court junction and take the Goshree road over the bridge to Bolgatty.

The airport & Aluva

Take NH 544 south to Edappally, then into the city towards High Court junction and over the Goshree bridge. Allow about an hour.

Fort Kochi & Vypin

Cross to Vypin and take the Goshree road south to the Bolgatty turn. Allow extra time for evening traffic.
